# Zava Return Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-01_

## 1. Return window
- **Standard items:** 30 days from delivery for a full refund.
- **Live plants and seeds:** 14 days from delivery. Must be reported with photo evidence.
- **Clearance / final-sale items:** No returns. Marked clearly at checkout.

## 2. Condition
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags/labels intact.
- Damaged or defective items are eligible for free replacement or refund at Zava's discretion.

## 3. How to start a return
1. Visit `https://zava.example/orders` and select the order.
2. Choose "Return item" and follow the prompts.
3. Print the prepaid shipping label emailed to you within 24 hours.
4. Drop the package at any authorized carrier location.

## 4. Refund timing
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** of Zava receiving the returned item.
- Zava Rewards points earned on the returned order are reversed at the same time.

## 5. Exceptions
- Gift cards, digital downloads, and installation services are **non-refundable**.
- Composting equipment on backorder can be cancelled anytime before it ships for a full refund.

## 6. Contact
For return questions, contact `support@zava.example` or use the in-app assistant.
